## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a hyper-focused, premium industrial atmosphere tailored for high-end electrical engineering and infrastructure services. The visual direction balances the precision of critical utility work with the sleek, high-tech confidence of modern engineering.

### Brand Identity
- **Personality:** Decisive, authoritative, technologically advanced, and rigorously dependable.
- **Target Audience:** Commercial project managers, industrial contractors, architectural engineers, and high-demand residential clients seeking flawless electrical craftsmanship.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires absolute safety, technical mastery, and kinetic precision through an electric, high-contrast dark environment.

### Design Movement: High-Contrast Precision Dark
Drawing from technical minimalism and luminous industrial UI, the interface utilizes pure charcoal and carbon foundations pierced by intense, energetic crimson highlights. Depth is achieved not through blurry ornamentation, but through surgical borders, subtle luminous glows (halation), and layered, matte-finished surfaces.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for visual hierarchy and optimal legibility in a dark environment, reserving saturated crimson strictly for focal elements and kinetic feedback.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Canvas (`#0A0A0A`):** Pure deep charcoal grounding the main application workspace.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#121212`):** Primary card and panel backdrop, establishing structure without stark separation.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#1C1917`):** Elevated modals, dropdowns, and active card segments with a faint warm carbon undertone.
- **Primary Crimson (`#DC2626`):** High-energy electric red dedicated to primary CTAs, critical indicators, and primary visual anchors.
- **Secondary Crimson (`#EF4444`):** High-visibility red used for interactive hover states, active badges, and focused accents.
- **Tertiary Maroon (`#991B1B`):** Deep structural red for subtle borders, badges with low visual noise, and container tints.
- **Text & Foreground:** High-luminance stark white (`#F9FAFB`) for headlines and interactive typography; muted steel gray (`#9CA3AF`) for body content and metadata to prevent eye fatigue.

## Typography

The pairing combines the architectural, geometric precision of **Space Grotesk** with the balanced, highly legible utility of **Manrope**.

- **Display & Headings:** `Space Grotesk` introduces technical rigor, reminiscent of blueprints, circuit diagrams, and precision equipment readouts. Its subtle geometric quirks lend an unmistakably modern, engineered signature.
- **Body & Continuous Reading:** `Manrope` provides neutral, open counters and high legibility across dark surfaces, preventing optical vibration often found with high-contrast text.
- **Labels & Overlines:** Rendered in uppercase `Space Grotesk` with expanded letter-spacing to emulate technical terminal callouts and safety-grade industrial labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored in an 8pt architectural rhythm, maintaining clear structure and generous breathing room to balance deep tonal backdrops.

### Grid & Responsiveness
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid, 24px gutters, max-width container capped at 1280px, with 48px outer margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout, 20px gutters, 32px canvas margins.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column layout, 16px gutters, 20px outer margins. Multi-column cards collapse into a direct, single-column vertical stack.

### Spacing Principles
- Layout structures favor vertical rhythmic clarity. Form controls and actionable tiles rely on consistent `space-md` (16px) internal padding, while card blocks enforce `space-lg` (24px) breathing space to isolate technical specifications cleanly.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on soft ambient lighting; instead, it uses crisp layered surfaces, subtle rim outlines, and intentional crimson energy blooms.

### Stratification Model
1. **Base Layer (`#0A0A0A`):** The primary canvas plane.
2. **Elevated Tier 1 (`#121212`):** Cards, panels, and sideboards bordered by a hairline stroke (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
3. **Elevated Tier 2 (`#1C1917`):** Menus, dialogs, and flyouts bordered with high-contrast precision (`1px solid rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.25)`).

### Halation & Shadows
- **Standard Elevators:** Deep, non-tinted cast shadows (`0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`).
- **Luminous Crimson Halation:** Used strictly on focal active states and primary CTA elements to evoke high voltage: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -2px rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.45)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft** geometry (`roundedness: 1`), keeping elements crisp and mechanical rather than overly organic or playful.

- **Standard Elements (4px / 0.25rem):** Buttons, inputs, inline badges, and chips. The tight corner radii evoke machined metal panels and industrial enclosures.
- **Containers & Cards (8px / 0.5rem):** Panels, dialogs, and large tiles retain controlled corners that maintain structural integrity.
- **Avatars & Status Dots:** Maintained as strict full circles (`rounded-full`) to immediately differentiate live telemetry and user indicators from structural components.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in `#DC2626`, label in `#FFFFFF`, with a 1px border of `#EF4444`. Hover activates a subtle outer glow (`0 0 16px rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.5)`) and darkens surface to `#B91C1C`.
- **Secondary:** Surface `#121212`, 1px border in `#27272A`, text in `#F9FAFB`. Hover shifts border to `#DC2626` and text to `#FFFFFF`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background, text `#9CA3AF`. Hover transitions text to `#DC2626` with no container fill.

### Badges & Chips
- **Status Indicators (High Voltage / Certified):** Background `#1C1917`, hairline border `#DC2626`, typography in `label-sm` uppercase with `#EF4444` fill. Include an optional 6px pulsing crimson LED indicator on the left edge.
- **Filter Chips:** Muted dark `#121212` background. Active state toggles to a solid `#DC2626` with white text.

### Form Inputs
- **Base State:** Background `#0E0E0E`, border `1px solid #27272A`, text `#F9FAFB`, placeholder `#6B7280`.
- **Focus State:** Border sharply transitions to `1px solid #DC2626`, accompanied by a minimal focus ring (`0 0 0 1px #DC2626`).

### Cards & Panels
- **Standard Card:** Background `#121212`, stroke `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
- **Interactive Card:** When hovered, the top border illuminates with a 2px Crimson accent bar (`#DC2626`) and lifts the background to `#1C1917`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Elements
- **Unchecked:** Flat charcoal base `#121212` with a `1px solid #3F3F46` frame.
- **Checked:** Filled with `#DC2626` featuring an angular white vector tick, reinforcing an assertive, confirmed state.

### Specialty: Electrical Metrics & Load Monologue
- **Telemetry Display:** Dark inset panel (`#070707`) with high-contrast monospace readings in white, complemented by an active load meter in electric crimson to communicate real-time power metrics and project specifications cleanly.
